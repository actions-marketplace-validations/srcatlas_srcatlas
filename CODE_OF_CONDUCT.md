# Code of Conduct

This project follows the principles of the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1 (https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). This is a short statement of those principles for this project; the full Covenant text applies where this file is silent.

## Our standard

Expected behaviour:
- Be respectful and constructive. Disagree about ideas, not people.
- Give and accept feedback on code and docs directly and kindly.
- Assume good faith; ask before concluding.
- Keep discussion on the project's topics.

Unacceptable behaviour:
- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory language or jokes.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention.
- Personal or political attacks, trolling, or deliberate derailing.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Anything else that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews) and when someone represents the project in public.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, issues and other contributions that violate this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban a person from the project.

Report violations privately to: **<lenard.tunya@outlook.com>**. Reports are handled confidentially. The maintainer who receives a report will not take part in deciding about it if they are the subject of it, and will ask another maintainer or an external reviewer to do so.
